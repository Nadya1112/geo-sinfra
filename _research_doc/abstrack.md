**ABSTRAK**

Pengelolaan data infrastruktur permukiman di Dinas Perumahan Rakyat dan Kawasan Permukiman (DPRKP) Kota Banjarmasin masih dihadapkan pada kendala pengolahan data yang belum terintegrasi, identifikasi kondisi fisik secara manual, serta penentuan prioritas pembangunan yang rentan akan subjektivitas. Kondisi tersebut menyebabkan proses *monitoring* dan pengambilan keputusan eksekutif menjadi kurang efektif. Penelitian ini bertujuan untuk merancang Sistem Informasi Geografis (SIG) cerdas berbasis *web* yang mengintegrasikan data spasial dan teknis *Detail Engineering Design* (DED) untuk menghasilkan klasifikasi kondisi infrastruktur dan rekomendasi prioritas pembangunan secara otomatis. Klasifikasi cerdas dibangun menggunakan ekstraksi fitur visual *Convolutional Neural Network* (CNN) arsitektur ResNet-18 dan algoritma *Decision Tree*. Penelitian ini juga menerapkan teknik *Synthetic Minority Over-sampling Technique* (SMOTE) untuk mengatasi ketidakseimbangan kelas (*class imbalance*) dan *Principal Component Analysis* (PCA) untuk reduksi dimensi fitur. Sistem dikembangkan menggunakan metode *Waterfall*, dieksekusi di atas *framework* Laravel dan basis data MySQL, serta memanfaatkan Leaflet.js sebagai instrumen visualisasi spasial interaktif. Hasil pengujian performa algoritma cerdas menunjukkan keandalan yang sangat tinggi dengan tingkat akurasi mencapai 94,20%, *precision* 95,38%, *recall* 96,88%, dan *F1-Score* sebesar 96,12%. Selain itu, pengujian reliabilitas *website* menggunakan GTMetrix membuktikan sistem berjalan optimal (predikat *Grade B*, *Structure Score* 96%), sedangkan pengujian pengalaman pengguna menghasilkan nilai *System Usability Scale* (SUS) sebesar 83,18 yang masuk dalam kategori *Acceptable* dengan predikat *Excellent* (*Grade B*). Kesimpulannya, platform SIG GEO-SINFRA yang dikembangkan terbukti mampu mendukung proses klasifikasi kondisi infrastruktur secara objektif, menunjang keputusan prioritas rehabilitasi yang tepat sasaran, serta meningkatkan efektivitas tata kelola ekosistem kota cerdas di lingkungan DPRKP Kota Banjarmasin.

**Kata Kunci:** Sistem Informasi Geografis, *Convolutional Neural Network*, *Decision Tree*, SMOTE, *System Usability Scale*, Infrastruktur Permukiman.

**ABSTRACT**

The management of residential infrastructure data at the Department of Housing and Settlement Areas (DPRKP) of Banjarmasin City still faces several challenges, including the lack of integrated data management, manual physical condition identification, and subjective determination of development priorities. These issues reduce the effectiveness of monitoring and executive decision-making processes. This study aims to design and develop an intelligent web-based Geographic Information System (GIS) that integrates spatial data and Detailed Engineering Design (DED) technical data to automatically generate infrastructure condition classifications and development priority recommendations. The intelligent classification model was built using the Convolutional Neural Network (CNN) ResNet-18 architecture for visual feature extraction and the Decision Tree algorithm. This study also implemented the Synthetic Minority Over-sampling Technique (SMOTE) to address class imbalance and Principal Component Analysis (PCA) for feature dimensionality reduction. The system was developed using the Waterfall methodology, running on the Laravel framework and MySQL database, and utilizing Leaflet.js for interactive spatial visualization. The intelligent algorithm performance evaluation demonstrated exceptional reliability, achieving an accuracy of 94.20%, precision of 95.38%, recall of 96.88%, and an F1-Score of 96.12%. Furthermore, the website reliability testing using GTMetrix proved that the system runs optimally (Grade B, Structure Score 96%), while the user experience evaluation resulted in a System Usability Scale (SUS) score of 83.18, falling into the Acceptable category with an Excellent rating (Grade B). In conclusion, the developed GEO-SINFRA GIS platform has proven capable of supporting objective infrastructure condition classifications, assisting in accurately targeted rehabilitation priority decisions, and improving the effectiveness of smart city ecosystem governance within the DPRKP of Banjarmasin City.

**Keywords:** Geographic Information System, Convolutional Neural Network, Decision Tree, SMOTE, System Usability Scale, Residential Infrastructure.
